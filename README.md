<p>Week 1 Task 3 - Some of the challenges with engaging in music data could be that full access is often blocked with a paywall or advertisements. Sites such as YouTube or platforms like ITunes and Spotify will often have what you want to hear, but not free or at the cost of your time. Creating your own music data to be distributed on these platforms can also be difficult - certain copyright laws making the distribution process longer and in the long run not worth it. Despite these challenges, the way data is preserved today is useful for accessing older pieces of music, or music originally not preserved digitally. My theme (the Revolve Album) is preserved predominantly online, through videos on YouTube, access to tracks in Spotify (and other similar platforms). Further, works of the Beatles have been consistently analysed by many people - published books being available in places such as Waterstones, journals and fan collaboration becoming available online, and the overall presence of these artists staying iconic and well known in online spaces decades later.</p>
